# Commercial Licensing

Jura Trace is published under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later)**. This means anyone may use, modify, study, and redistribute the software, provided they comply with the AGPL's terms.

For most users — journalists, researchers, human rights workers, legal professionals, academic users, non-profit organisations, individual professionals — the AGPL is all you need. Continue to use Jura Trace freely.

## When you might need a commercial licence

The AGPL has two requirements that some commercial use cases cannot accept:

1. **Source disclosure on network use.** If you operate a modified version of Jura Trace as a hosted service that users connect to over a network, the AGPL requires you to make the modified source code available to those users.

2. **Copyleft on derivative works.** If you incorporate Jura Trace into a larger product, the AGPL requires that larger product to also be distributed under AGPL-compatible terms.

If your operation cannot meet either of those conditions — for example, you need to integrate Jura Trace into a closed-source product, deploy a modified version internally without making source available, or you require contractual indemnification beyond what the AGPL provides — a commercial licence is available.

## How to request a commercial licence

Email **licensing@juralabs.org** with:

- A short description of the intended use
- The scale of the deployment (number of users, number of organisations)
- Whether indemnification is required
- Any additional terms your organisation needs

## What is not on offer

- **SaaS hosting of Jura Trace by Jura Labs.** The product is local-first by design. We do not run a hosted service.
- **Reseller rights** — a separate distribution licence is required.
- **Custom feature development** — handled as a separate engagement, not bundled with the licence.

## Why dual-licensing

The AGPL ensures that the source code and any modifications remain available to the public — particularly important for a forensic verification tool whose claims should be auditable. The commercial licence captures revenue from organisations that cannot operate under copyleft terms, which funds continued development of the free version. Both audiences are served.
