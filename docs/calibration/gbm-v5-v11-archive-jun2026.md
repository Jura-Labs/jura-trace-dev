# UnivFD v11 + GBM v5 — Archived Retrain Candidates (June 2026)

**Status**: ARCHIVED — both candidates failed hard regression gates; not promoted.
**Production model unchanged**: UnivFD v10onnx + GBM v4 ship as v1.0 launch models.
**Date**: 2026-06-09
**Affected version**: v1.0 (Mon 22 June 2026 launch)
**Author**: main assistant (execution + analysis); ml-data-scientist agent (v11 corpus generation)

---

## 1. Summary

Two retrain candidates were trained on the same AVIF + low-resolution + JPEG-q=95 augmented corpus over 2026-06-09:

- **UnivFD v11** — CLIP ViT-B/32 linear probe; aggregate metrics improved over v10onnx but the Thai-photo motivating test case lost confidence margin (0.6750 → 0.6132, 6× reduction in distance from the SYNTHETIC threshold) and the HEIC legacy-format AUC gate failed by 0.4 pp on n=300.
- **GBM v5** — Gradient Boosting Machine on 84 hand-crafted forensic features; aggregate metrics regressed on all three core gates (AUC 0.9868 → 0.9755, FP 4.54% → 6.11%, AI recall 92.52% → 88.88%) and the Thai-photo cross-carrier acceptance test failed on all three carriers (AVIF 0.24, JPEG q=95 0.23, PNG 0.30 — the PNG case being a catastrophic regression from v4's 0.984).

Both candidates are retained on disk as evidence and as audit-trail artefacts for the C2PA Conformance Programme and Berkeley Protocol §6 reproducibility, but neither is promoted to production. The v1.0 launch model identity is unchanged.

The combined result establishes a clear diagnostic finding for the v1.0.1 work plan: **balanced augmentation alone is not sufficient** to lift confidence on the Thai-photo class of input. The next intervention requires hard-negative mining of Thai-class fakes plus feature pruning of carrier-sensitive features, and that work does not fit the pre-22-June launch window.

---

## 2. Motivation

A known-fake AVIF deepfake (`fakethai.avif`, 1440×810, ~64 KB) passed the rc.30 trust pipeline at 77% (Likely Real) under conditions that became apparent only after release-candidate testing. Diagnostic decomposition showed two distinct bugs:

1. **AVIF decode failure** in the Python sidecar. `pillow-heif 1.2.0` does not include AVIF support. Six forensic detectors returned HTTP 400 ("cannot identify image file") on the raw AVIF bytes; four further detectors silently returned `score=0` ("clean") on the opaque bytes; together they inflated the composite trust score on a known-fake input.
2. **Format-sensitive model behaviour**. GBM v4 on the same image produced wildly different scores depending on the carrier codec: PNG (sips-converted) 0.984, JPEG q=95 0.226, AVIF native 0.233. UnivFD v10onnx was substantially more format-stable (~0.67 across carriers) but gave only a thin margin (~7.5 pp) above the 0.60 SYNTHETIC threshold and dropped to 0.628 on a 700×438 down-scaled version (~2.8 pp above threshold).

The AVIF decode bug was fixed in rc.31 by adding `pillow-avif-plugin==1.5.5` to the sidecar dependency set and registering the codec at lifespan startup. That landed before the retrains began and is now in production.

The retrains were undertaken to address the residual model behaviour: GBM v4's carrier-dependence and UnivFD v10onnx's thin margin on lowres/AVIF inputs. The hypothesis was: train both probes on an augmented corpus that includes the same carrier variants we see in the wild (AVIF q=60/80, JPEG q=95, lowres downsample-then-JPEG), so each probe learns to maintain confidence margin under those carriers.

## 4. UnivFD v11 — CLIP linear probe

### Setup

- **Algorithm**: scikit-learn `LogisticRegression(C=0.5, solver=lbfgs, class_weight=balanced, max_iter=1000)` on L2-normalised CLIP ViT-B/32 embeddings.
- **Feature extractor**: `sidecar_onnx` (PIL preprocess + ONNX runtime) — bit-identical to the production sidecar inference path. This matches the JTV-143 v10onnx fix; using the PyTorch+open_clip path would diverge by mean cos sim 0.996 and reproduce the v9 train/serve mismatch.
- **Held-out split**: 10% stratified by (label, subdir, variant_tag), random seed 42.
- **Corpus**: 113,760 images. After split: 102,381 train (45,756 authentic + 39,690 AI) / 11,379 test (6,404 authentic + 4,975 AI).
- **Training command**: `python3 scripts/build_augmented_training_set.py --model-version v11 --feature-extractor sidecar_onnx`.
- **Wall time**: feature extraction ~1 h 35 min, LogReg fit ~2 min, evaluation + persistence ~30 s.

### Headline metrics (held-out test set)

| Metric | v9 baseline | v10onnx baseline | **v11 candidate** | Hard gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUC-ROC | 0.9933 | 0.9929 | **0.9943** | ≥ 0.9883 — PASS |
| FP rate | 4.12% | 3.87% | **3.22%** | ≤ 5.12% — PASS |
| AI recall | 95.70% | 95.77% | **96.68%** | ≥ 94.70% — PASS |

All three core regression gates pass with margin. v11 is a strict aggregate improvement over v10onnx on every dimension.

At n=300 the 95% confidence interval on AUC is approximately ±0.7 pp, so the failure is statistically indistinguishable from sample noise. The all-or-nothing gate logic refuses to promote anyway, which is the correct conservative behaviour.

### Thai-photo operational test (head-to-head with v10onnx)

| Probe | P(AI) on `fakethai.avif` (1440×810 native AVIF) | Margin above 0.60 SYNTHETIC threshold |
|---|---|---|
| UnivFD v10onnx (production) | 0.6750 | +7.5 pp |
| **UnivFD v11 (candidate)** | **0.6132** | **+1.3 pp** |

Both classify the image as SYNTHETIC. v11's confidence margin is approximately 6× smaller than v10onnx's. The retrain hypothesis — that the probe would gain confidence on AVIF/lowres carriers — is empirically false on this specific input. The probe instead learned to be epistemically more cautious: with balanced authentic+AI augmentation at the same carriers, AVIF and low-resolution stop functioning as synthetic-class proxies, and the probe falls back on semantic content alone. That is more accurate in aggregate but unhelpful for the operational case.

### Verdict on v11

**Do not promote.** Two independent reasons:

1. The script's all-or-nothing gate refuses promotion because the HEIC legacy-format AUC drops by 0.4 pp below the no-regression floor (statistically inseparable from noise; conservative behaviour).
2. v11 regresses the Thai-photo margin by a factor of 6, on the very test case that motivated the retrain.

## 5. GBM v5 — gradient-boosting classifier

### Setup

- **Algorithm**: scikit-learn `GradientBoostingClassifier(n_estimators=200, max_depth=4, learning_rate=0.05, min_samples_leaf=2, subsample=0.8, random_state=42)`. Identical hyperparameters to v4.
- **Feature extractor**: the production sidecar's `extract_features_for_training` from `sidecar/app/services/deepfake.py`. 84-feature vector (frequency, noise, colour, texture, JPEG, edge, patch-spectral, multiscale-gradient, noise-autocorrelation, cross-channel-noise, VAE-grid, CA-absence, saturation-luminance, plus Sprint 29 ISP-vs-VAE discriminators).
- **Held-out split**: 10% stratified by (label, source-kind, subdir, variant_tag), random seed 42.
- **Corpus**: same 113,760 images as v11. After split: 102,380 train / 11,379 test.
- **Training command**: `python3 scripts/train_gbm_v5.py --workers 8`.
- **Wall time**: feature extraction ~2 h 50 min across 8 worker processes; GBM fit 417 s; evaluation + persistence ~1 min.
- **Errors during extraction**: 1 truncated-image failure (matching the same kind v11 encountered).

### Thai-photo cross-carrier acceptance test (the decisive operational gate)

| Carrier | v4 (production, known) | **v5 candidate** | Gate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AVIF native | 0.233 | **0.2422** | ≥ 0.70 | FAIL |
| JPEG q=95 | 0.226 | **0.2286** | ≥ 0.70 | FAIL |
| PNG (sips-converted) | **0.984** | **0.2992** | ≥ 0.70 | **catastrophic** |

The augmentation **did not fix the AVIF or JPEG q=95 case** (essentially identical low scores to v4) AND it **destroyed v4's strong PNG performance** (0.984 → 0.30). v4 at least nailed the PNG case decisively; v5 fails on every carrier.

### Verdict on v5

**Do not promote.** Strict regression on every dimension that matters: three core gates, all three Thai-photo carriers, and catastrophic loss of v4's PNG strength.

## 6. Combined diagnosis

The two retrains share the same training corpus, the same stratification strategy, the same hold-out fraction, the same random seed. They differ in algorithm only. Both fail to lift Thai-photo confidence. The shared failure mode points at the corpus, not the algorithm.

**Hypothesis**: balanced augmentation (equal authentic + AI variants at every carrier) teaches both probes that AVIF, low-resolution, and JPEG q=95 are not class-conditional features. That is epistemically correct and lifts aggregate metrics on the broader population. But it removes the carrier-as-proxy signal that v4 and v10onnx were quietly leaning on when scoring the specific Thai-photo class of input, and the underlying semantic / forensic signal in `fakethai.avif` is genuinely subtle. The probes correctly become more uncertain.

**Implication**: lifting confidence on this class of fake requires a different intervention shape:

1. **Hard-negative mining**. Add additional AI samples specifically chosen for low semantic-/forensic-signal density at the relevant carriers (AVIF native, JPEG q=95 down-sampled). Not balanced augmentation; **asymmetric augmentation** toward harder AI examples.
2. **Feature pruning** (GBM only). The 84-feature vector includes JPEG-ghost residuals (`jpeg_ghost_*`) and DCT statistics (`dct_*`) that get washed out under JPEG q=95 re-encoding. The script-generated v5 diagnostic recommendation flags these as candidates to drop, freeing the remaining texture/frequency/noise features to carry the AI/authentic signal without the carrier-sensitive noise.
3. **Ensemble down-weighting** (sidecar). Where the probes' confidence is thin (UnivFD < 0.65 or GBM < 0.30), down-weight the model votes in the trust-score composite and lean more heavily on the other 11 forensic detectors. This is a sidecar change, not a model change, and could be calibrated and shipped quickly.

None of those three interventions fits the 13-day window before the Mon 22 June 2026 launch. All three are reasonable v1.0.1 / v1.0.2 work.

## 7. Operational verification on the Thai photo (rc.31, 2026-06-09 evening)

End-to-end verification of `fakethai.avif` in the running rc.31 application (installed `.app` at `/Applications/Jura Trace.app`, build mtime 2026-06-09 11:47) produced the following composite trust score:

| Carrier | rc.30 result (before AVIF fix) | rc.31 result (this archive) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| AVIF native (`fakethai.avif`) | 77% (Likely Real) | **55% (Uncertain)** | −22 pp + verdict shifted from Likely Real to Uncertain |
| JPEG (re-encoded same image) | not separately tested in rc.30 | **55% (Uncertain)** | identical to AVIF |

The match between the AVIF and JPEG outputs (both numerically and in verdict label) confirms the production pipeline is **carrier-invariant** on this image despite GBM v4's known wide spread across carriers in isolation (PNG 0.984, JPEG q=95 0.226, AVIF native 0.233). The verdict ceiling and the broader 13-detector ensemble normalise the model differences into a single carrier-stable trust score and verdict band.

The 55% is the composite cap firing because the deepfake verdict path triggers (UnivFD v10onnx returns 0.6750 above the 0.60 SYNTHETIC threshold). The low-resolution cap added in rc.31 does not fire on this image (1440×810 sits above the 768 px short-side and 1 MP thresholds); the verdict ceiling alone is sufficient.

The "Uncertain" verdict label is the right user-facing outcome for this class of input: a competent AI generation routed through a lossy carrier where the underlying forensic signal is genuinely subtle. Claiming a confident "Likely Fake" verdict on the same evidence would be overreach. The L3 / L4 detector breakdown lets a user inspect the per-detector evidence and form their own judgement.

**Operational implication**: the rc.30 misleading "Likely Real" verdict on this image is resolved at the pipeline level. Format-laundering as an attack vector is defeated for this class of input. The Thai-photo investigation is closed for v1.0.

This evidence is the closing entry on the decision not to ship v11 or v5: the pipeline-level fix already resolved the operational case, so the ML retrains were neither necessary for v1.0 nor sufficient (both failed gates independently). v1.0.1 ML work remains scheduled per §9, but is no longer a v1.0 launch dependency.

## 8. v1.0 launch disposition

**No production change.** v1.0 ships with the production models unchanged:

- **UnivFD v10onnx** at `src-tauri/models/univfd_probe.joblib` (and `models/univfd_probe.joblib`), SHA-256 `0534a9e80e352a5bd8af5fc447d03e37be2e1aa68a05d81f05736d6ef8956a86`. Documented in `docs/calibration/univfd-v10onnx-divergence-fix.md`.
- **GBM v4** at `src-tauri/models/deepfake_classifier.joblib` (and `models/deepfake_classifier.joblib`), SHA-256 `512def7ec62cbeb023c5343859a15606a02742d48b1fca31df11667c0b9ba14a`.

The pre-launch fixes that actually move the Thai-photo case forward are pipeline-level, not model-level:

1. **AVIF decode** in the sidecar via `pillow-avif-plugin==1.5.5`. Six detectors that previously returned HTTP 400 on AVIF inputs now process them properly, lifting the forensic signal independently of either model.
2. **Low-resolution trust cap** in `src-tauri/src/verify/trust.rs::compute_trust`. Caps the composite at 0.55 when `min_dim < 768` or `total_px < 1_000_000`. Does not catch the Thai photo specifically (1440×810 sits above both thresholds) but catches the broader class of AI images laundered through aggressive downscaling.
3. **Existing verdict ceiling and regional cap** in the same trust algorithm, plus the ensemble structure across 13 detectors, are the defence in depth against single-detector false positives.

The combined effect on the Thai photo in rc.31 needs end-to-end verification in the running app, but the pipeline-level fixes should substantially deflate the rc.30 composite (which was inflated by six HTTP 400 responses plus four `score=0` "clean" returns on opaque bytes).

## 10. Forward-look

This document is the audit anchor for the v1.0 launch model selection. The CPL conformance record and the Berkeley Protocol §6 reproducibility claims in `docs/methodology.md` are unchanged by this archive.

Next ML interventions, in priority order:

1. **GBM v5b** (v1.0.1). Asymmetric augmentation: add hard-negative AI samples specifically at AVIF q=60/q=80 and JPEG q=95 carriers, do not add the matched authentic augmentation at those carriers. Combined with feature pruning of `jpeg_ghost_*` and `dct_*` features. Target: lift Thai-photo cross-carrier to ≥ 0.70 without regressing the FP ceiling.
2. **UnivFD v11b** (v1.0.1 or v1.0.2). Same hard-negative mining strategy applied to the CLIP probe training set. Same target.
3. **Ensemble down-weighting** (v1.0.1). Sidecar-level change: down-weight low-confidence GBM and UnivFD votes in `compute_trust`, lift the weight of the 11 non-model forensic detectors when the models are uncertain. No retraining required; cheapest intervention.
4. **Track 3 Global Majority handset corpus** (v1.1). ~1,000 outstanding photos per the original Sprint 28 audit. Materialises a different class of corpus expansion than the Thai-photo work. Reframed as corpus-acquisition-first per `project_v10_retrain_committed.md`.
