Jura Trace protects your images, documents, video, audio, and 3D models from unauthorised AI scraping. It helps communities verify the truth of what they see. Everything runs locally on your machine. Nothing leaves.

Local-first by design. No cloud. No accounts. No telemetry. Your files never leave this device.
The Problem

AI companies are scraping your content at industrial scale — your photographs, your research, your art, your archives, your recordings.

Generative AI models are trained on content taken without consent. Artists lose control of their style. Publishers see their work reproduced without attribution. Researchers find their papers ingested into systems they never authorised. Communities are flooded with synthetic media they cannot distinguish from reality. The tools to fight back should not require a cloud subscription or an engineering degree.

P

Protect

Defend your content before it reaches the open web. Embed provenance, apply invisible watermarks, and fingerprint every asset so you can prove ownership later.

  • AI auto-cataloguing for bulk processing
  • C2PA Content Credentials signing
  • Perceptual fingerprinting across all formats
  • Invisible watermarking that survives compression
  • Machine-readable rights and AI opt-out manifests
  • Batch processing: thousands of files overnight
V

Verify

Check whether an image has been manipulated, whether a video is a deepfake, or whether a claim circulating online is supported by evidence.

  • Error Level Analysis and clone detection
  • AI deepfake detection (image, video, audio)
  • Full EXIF and metadata forensics
  • C2PA provenance chain verification
  • Claim checking against fact-check databases
  • Trust reports with sourced evidence
M

Monitor

Track where your content appears online. Get alerts when your fingerprinted assets are found in unexpected places. Build evidence for legal action.

  • Scrape detection and alert system
  • Fingerprint match tracking across the web
  • EU AI Act transparency compliance reports
  • Evidence packaging for legal proceedings
  • Institutional dashboard and audit trail
  • Export-ready documentation
