# BL-DIST-002: Jura Trace is not on the Digital Public Goods registry

**Status**: Open. Target v1.3.0 for the product and documentation changes
the application needs (Paul, 22 October 2026). The application itself is
an outward-facing act and waits for Paul.
**Raised**: 22 October 2026, from the pre-v1.3.0 adoption review.
**Severity**: Medium for reach: the registry is read by UN agencies and
Global Majority funders.

## What the application needs

From the review's reading of digitalpublicgoods.net (submission guide and
the standard; not re-read): nine indicators, a review of about 30 days,
renewal each year. Gaps in Trace:

1. **Privacy (indicators 7 and 9A).** A privacy policy for the app itself,
   covering what it does not collect, the false-positive report email
   flow, API keys and bring-your-own-key provider keys (BL-RIS-001), and
   what Enhanced mode sends. juralabs.org/privacy exists (200, 22 Oct) and
   needs checking against the app.
2. **Ownership (indicator 3).** A public statement of who owns the code
   and under what terms. Paul owns the IP personally; the CIC publishes
   under a perpetual licence; a Danish business handles commercial
   licences. This needs Paul's wording.
3. **Open data and standards (indicator 6).** Document that results are
   exportable in open formats (JSON, the Verification Record, C2PA).
4. **Licence.** AGPL-3.0-or-later; the review found it on a DPGA licence
   list in a help file, authoritative status unconfirmed.
5. **Do no harm.** A short statement of misuse risks and mitigations
   (false positives, the second-opinion note, no verdicts by language
   models).

## What not to do

- Do not submit until Paul has approved the ownership wording.
